# v0.9.0 Release Readiness

## Product

Risk Model + Constrained Minimum Variance 1.0.

## Supported methods

- Equal Weight
- Rank Weight
- Inverse Volatility
- Minimum Variance

Minimum Variance supports Sample Covariance and Ledoit-Wolf risk estimators and
the existing Max Weight constraint.

## Frozen semantics

- Top-N owns selection. Portfolio Construction may only map weights onto the
  exact selected identity and order; it cannot add, drop, substitute, or rerank.
- Historical covariance uses one common complete-case matrix, exact requested
  asset order, daily unannualized returns, and sample `ddof=1` where selected.
- Pairwise covariance, generic missing-value filling, covariance repair, and
  hidden lookback expansion are forbidden.
- Covariance is validated as symmetric and positive semidefinite; validation
  does not clip eigenvalues or otherwise repair the matrix.
- Minimum Variance is long-only and fully invested. SLSQP solves the constraints
  directly, including Max Weight bounds, with no post-solve clipping,
  normalization, candidate replacement, or fallback.
- A selected security with zero target weight remains a canonical Holdings row.
- Risk cutoff is the latest open date on or before formation. Future returns do
  not influence covariance, weights, or Holdings.
- The generic service graph is `minimum_variance -> risk_model ->
  historical_returns`; run-scoped dependencies are constructed once.

## Artifacts and pipeline

There is no Risk Model Artifact. Holdings remains exactly
`trade_date, ts_code, target_weight, score, rank`, and its schema/version and
four-file Artifact set are unchanged. Pipeline stages and summaries are
unchanged. Research Backtest continues to consume only canonical Holdings
`target_weight`; its accounting and Artifact contracts are unchanged.

## Non-goals

- Expected returns or mean-variance optimization
- Risk parity
- Turnover constraints or pre-trade state
- Sector or factor constraints
- Tracking error
- Live execution
